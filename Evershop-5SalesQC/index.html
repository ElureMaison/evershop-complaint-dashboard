Notes on this data

Source: the 5+ Sales QC board (qualitycontrol-xi), read through its own API from a signed-in browser — the list plus each product's detail page. The board itself is the Notion "5+ sales quality control" database.
Entered QC is the board's date: the day a product reached 5 sales with live ad traffic. Outcomes for finished products: Passed clean (no edits), Fixed (listing edited after QC), Pulled / failed (discontinued, QC decision Failed, or the supplier could not source it).
Checks come from the AI QC reports run on each supplier's photos and size charts: material, images, functionality and size chart are each ok, mismatch or unknown; the ✗ rates count ok vs mismatch only. A report's verdict is pass, warn or fail.
Supplier is the board's supplier field, else the supplier QC was run against, else the Notion product board (29 Sep copy).
Refund impact matches products to the 💸 Refunds log by name (exact, then same brand with most words shared). The QC date is the 5th sale, so there are almost no refunds before it; each product is compared with itself instead — first 30 days after QC vs months 2–3. Sales volume also changes over that time and is not available here, so read these as counts, not refund rates.
Privacy: no customer data — product, supplier and QC results only.

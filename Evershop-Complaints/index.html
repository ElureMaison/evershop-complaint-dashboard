Notes on this data

Source: read directly from the Complaint Tracker's own published Google Sheets for each store (the same feeds the live dashboard renders from), captured via an authenticated session on 2026-09-03.
Stores: Clarendale and Lark & Clover keep separate complaint logs and separate product catalogs (different Shopify product IDs), so "per product" filtering is store-specific — the same physical item can appear under different names/IDs on each storefront and is not merged here.
Lark & Clover's complaint log starts 2026-04-30 (vs Clarendale's 2026-02-11) — it appears to be a newer or more recently-tracked storefront, which matters when comparing raw volumes between stores.
"GMV exposed" is the sum of order values for orders that generated a complaint — an exposure figure, not confirmed lost revenue.
Mistyped years are corrected on the fly: one Clarendale row (Glowkin – Transparent Zippered Pumpkin Favor Bags, order #2775927, placed 2026-09-10) is logged as 2020-09-19. Refresh re-dates any such row to the only year in the data's own range where that month and day fit, so it is counted in the right week rather than dropped. The cell in the sheet is still wrong; nothing on this page depends on it being fixed.
Response activity and out-of-stock detection are matched to complaint-log products by name, best-effort (not every row matches — shared ops sheets sometimes name products slightly differently than the complaint log); a miss just means that row is left out of these two sections, not double-counted elsewhere.

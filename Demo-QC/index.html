How this works

When a product reaches its 5th sale, suppliers are asked to quote it and send QC photos, a size chart and the material. It then moves through five lists:

  1. Supplier does QC: waiting for the supplier.
  2. We review QC: compare photos, size chart and cost; send questions.
  3. Supplier replies: waiting for answers and edits.
  4. Final updates: pick who fulfils it, fix the listing, set Done.
  5. Done.

Open any product to try it: send questions, pick a supplier, pass or fail it. ←/→ move between products, Esc closes, / searches.

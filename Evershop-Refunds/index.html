Notes on this data

Source: the Notion "💸 Refunds" database that the Solution tracker writes every refund into. The back end's own Refunds tab reads the same log.
Cost is the € refunded, as logged. Solution % is the share of the order refunded. A refund counts as off-ladder when its solution tag is not one of its funnel's steps (Funnel 1: 40/60/100 · Funnel 2: 20/30/40/50/100 · Funnel 3: 20/30/60/100 · Funnel 4: 20/30/50/100).
Dates: the Notion export prints dates without a year, so the year is inferred from each store's order-number sequence (the log runs from Jul 2025). A live feed from the Notion API carries full dates.
Products: add-ons (inspiration guides, tips, priority packing and insurance) are stripped. A multi-item refund is credited to its first product.
Reasons: older rows only have free text, mostly "Not satisfied", so they land in Not satisfied (no detail). Newer rows use the reason tags.
Privacy: no customer names, emails, addresses or ticket links are published — only order numbers.
